PSL Progress Tracker

Four weekly photo check-ins, one monthly trend (0–8 scale)
Scores are one AI model's subjective read of a photo, not a measurement of your worth or health, and PSL itself is a community-made scale rather than science. Lighting, angle, lens and expression move the number a lot, so use the same setup every week: neutral expression, front-facing, daylight, camera at eye level. For adults (18+) only. Everything runs on your computer: photos are analysed by a local model and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Each photo is measured with face landmarks and scored by the local model 3 times (median), which cuts noise a lot, but still treat changes under ~0.3 as noise.
Tier guide & how the score is computed

Overall = Harmony 40% + Dimorphism 25% + Angularity 20% + Miscellaneous 15%. Those weights are my choice; PSL has no official formula. Percentiles are not shown because there is no dataset of rated faces behind this app.

Harmony: features working together as a whole. Dimorphism: how masculine or feminine the features read. Angularity: sharpness of jaw, cheekbones and chin. Miscellaneous: skin, eyebrows, nose, teeth and smile.
